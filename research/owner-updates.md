# Owner updates received during research (2026-10-18)

1. **World scope: accurate San Francisco first.** The product owner clarified that one of the primary goals is an ACCURATE, recognizable representation of San Francisco (the city and county) that players will play in and mod. The rest of the Bay Area (Oakland, Berkeley, the Peninsula, San Jose, etc.) is deferred to later expansion milestones. Implication for the spec: R-MAP-01 becomes "SF accurate first"; world streaming, road graph, landmarks, neighborhoods, transit (Muni/BART stations as landmarks), bridges as boundaries/portals; East Bay/Peninsula appear as low-detail skyline/backdrop in v1 and as full districts in later milestones. Story Act 1 and the racing/fighter stages should be SF-only.
2. **Additional example repo: sharkpaw/Grand-V** (Turkish launcher + web overlay for SebasKitten/Grand-V, since SebasKitten's repo could not be cloned). Reusable logic: launcher hosting modes (local/LAN/public via Cloudflare tunnel), multiplayer relay, Mod Store (search/install/enable/disable, mod importer, gta5-mods.com metadata), save profiles, runtime i18n overlay that leaves upstream files untouched, transactional install/uninstall with backup, troubleshooting guide, Menyoo trainer menu tree (a checklist of sandbox features). The owner asked that its game-adjacent logic be used and translated to English. Report: research/repos-grand-v.md (being written by a separate agent). IP line unchanged: no engine/game data.

3. **fal.ai budget cap: $100 total (2026-10-18).** All fal spend (spikes, character pipeline, cutscenes, retakes) must not exceed USD 100 until the owner raises it. This replaces the G3 one-off production figure of $1,000. Recurring player-triggered fal spend stays $0.
4. **Cloudflare: create new resources for this project (2026-10-18).** The owner authorized new Workers/resources on their account. Created by the Architect: R2 `tha-bay-media-{staging,prod}`, `tha-bay-private-{staging,prod}`; KV `tha-bay-flags-{staging,prod}` (ids in `config/cloudflare.resources.json`). Worker deploys need `CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN` + `CLOUDFLARE_ACCOUNT_ID`.
