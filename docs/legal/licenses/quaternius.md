● Can these assets be used in commercial projects?
Yes , these assets can be used for free without the need for attribution in commercial, educational, and personal projects .
All models are under the CC0 License.
● What is the best way to stay informed about new releases?
You can join the Discord server or follow me on Twitter to receive notifications about future releases.
● Am I allowed to modify the models?
Yes , you are free to modify the models in any way you like, including combining them with other asset packs.
● Are the models textured?
This depends on the pack. Most packs are textured using an Atlas Texture , where a single image with colored squares is used to optimize performance.
Some packs may use individual materials instead which are also convenient for color variations.
● Do you offer services for custom asset creation?
Yes, you can request custom assets through my Patreon page.
● Is it necessary to give credi t when using these assets?
No, attribution is not necessary. However, credit is always appreciated .
These assets are licensed under CC0 , allowing you to use them without giving credit, even for commercial purposes.
● Which software can be used to edit these assets?
You can edit these models using Blender , which is available for free. You can open the .blend files directly or import the FBX files.
● How do I report a bug or issue with an asset?
You can contact me via the email provided at the bottom right of this website.
