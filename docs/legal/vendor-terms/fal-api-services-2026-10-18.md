“ Company Platform ” means Company’s generative media Service consisting of proprietary and third-party licensed machine learning and diffusion models, software, algorithms, workflows, methodologies, processes, systems, APIs, compute infrastructure, and other technology for providing artificial intelligence-based solutions.

“ End Users ” means customers of Client who use the Client Solution.
