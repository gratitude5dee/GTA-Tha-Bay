**Playwright test timeouts and retries (Builder-verified 2026-10-23, BD-FIX-CI7-F03).** (1) "Time spent by the test function, fixture setups, and `beforeEach` hooks is included in the test timeout", which is 30 seconds by default [https://playwright.dev/docs/test-timeouts, read 2026-10-23]: a slow `newContext` (the `context` fixture) uses up the budget of the test that asks for it.
