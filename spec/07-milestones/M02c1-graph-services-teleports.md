# M02c1 — graph-services-teleports

## Goal
After this session the baked city answers questions and moves the player. `@dv/world` implements the C08 service interfaces of `02-world-contracts.md` §3 in its sim half: `RoadGraphQuery` with A* over resident lanes and the always-resident `sf-graph.v1` topology, the junction runtime (signal aspects from the pure `signalAspect`), `TrafficControl` (blackout, density overrides, closures), the data side of `MapApi` (blips, waypoint, GPS route), `WorldQuery` (ground, water, neighbourhood and district lookups), `TeleportApi` (validate, pre-stream, fade, place), `WorldResolver` and `PlaceIndex` (landmarks, POIs, ped routes, place search over names). The bake writes `data/district-grid.v1`. One `WorldApi` object wires them together for the engine and, from M04a1, for `ModeContext.world`. The city also has edges: water and the county line are soft walls, the play bounds are a hard capsule wall, the Golden Gate and Bay Bridge are closed `Portal` entities, and a player in deep water swims and respawns on shore (DL-691). The player can teleport to any landmark from the sandbox list.

## Requirements
- R-MAP-01: positions, streets, neighbourhoods and landmarks resolve to the real baked geometry (`resolve`, `snap`, `nhoodAt`); bridges and the county line are boundaries and portals (`02-world.md` §1 item 23, `02-world-maps.md` §4.1–§4.2; DL-691).
- R-MAP-03: GPS routes, blips and teleports (sandbox and fast travel) exist as the services the minimap and full map (M02c2) draw.

## Depends on
- **M02a2:** road graph sections, junctions and signal programs, ped graph, tags, landmarks with arrival poses, POIs, `sf-graph.v1` and `graph-codec.ts`, `synth` with roads.
- **M02b1:** `ChunkStore` (residency, `needNow` requests), statics feed, world boot, `DV_WORLD=synth` CI builds.
- **M02b2:** the hashed blackout resource (adopted by `TrafficControl`, read by `signal()`), `__dvBridge.world.blackout` (DL-348).
- Contracts: C08 1.0 (`c08-api.ts` interfaces implemented verbatim; `signalAspect`, `chunkKeyOf`), C06 1.1 (`ModeContext.world: WorldApi`, implemented here and wired into the context later), C01 1.0 (Control states 700/701 for teleport).

## Scope in
- **Graph** (`packages/world/src/sim/graph/`): adjacency from resident `p0` EDGE and JUNCTION sections plus `sf-graph.v1`; A* with a binary heap, ties broken by edge id, `maxExpand`, `avoid`, `classMask`, `allowWrongWay`, `useTopology`; lane-level inside resident chunks and topology beyond, `complete: false` when the expansion cap stops the search; `etaS` from edge speeds; `laneCentre` per C08 rule 10; `nearestEdge` with optional `y` to separate layers; `resolve(lat, lng)` through the C08 frame constants; `signal(node, movement)` from `signalAspect(program, tick, movement, blackout)`; `neighbours`.
- **`TrafficControl`** (`src/sim/traffic/control.ts`): adopts M02b2's hashed blackout resource; `setDensity(kind, mult, by)` with 0–1.5 clamp and owner release; `closeEdges(edges, by)` marks edges closed for planning (A* avoids them); `release(by)`. Barrier props for closures attach in M03a1 (prefab `prop.barrier`).
- **`MapApi`** data (`src/sim/map/map-api.ts`): blip table with priorities, waypoint, `setRoute` (GPS polyline from A* on drivable classes), published as a view-model patch at `SimStage.Publish`; views are M02c2.
- **`WorldQuery`** (`src/sim/api/query.ts`): `groundY` from heightfield, slabs and hoods (`yHint` picks the layer), `isWater` from GROUND classes, `nhoodAt` and `districtAt` from `data/district-grid.v1` and `content/world/districts.json`, `chunkReady`, `nearestWalkable` on the ped graph.
- **District grid** (`tools/map/src/stages/s12-district-grid.ts`): 64 m cells over `play`, `u8` neighbourhood index (255 none), from the neighbourhood polygons fetched in M02a1; packed as `data/district-grid.v1` (`map:pack`), listed in the world index; reproducible.
- **`TeleportApi`** (`src/sim/api/teleport.ts`, `02-world-maps.md` §5): kinds `sandbox`, `fast-travel`, `xr-comfort`, `respawn`, `debug`; validation order mode rules, heat, bounds, cooldown (30 s fast travel); a `TeleportPolicy` hook decides mode rules (default: the session is sandbox until ModeHost lands in M04a2; heat is 0 until M03c); targets `{x, z, yaw?}`, `{landmark}` (arrival pose) or `{node}` (`teleportNodes`, transit entrances); pre-stream the destination `p0` tile plus a one-tile ring as `needNow`, wait ≤ 8 s, else `not-loaded` with `world.teleport.notLoaded` and the player unmoved; fade out ≤ 200 ms, Control 700, colliders wait (701); place by casting down from `y + 300 m`, reject water (`water`) and prisms (`blocked`) by searching the nearest valid arrival or walkable point within 40 m; fade in. Fast-travel fares (`config/world/transit.json`, integer cents) are recorded but not charged until `EconomyApi` lands (M04a2). Message keys go to `packages/ui/src/i18n/en.json`.
- **`WorldResolver`** (`src/sim/api/resolver.ts`): `bake` = `WorldIndex.bakeId`; `toEnu`; `landmark(id)` with chunk key; `snap` with modes `ground`, `road`, `sidewalk`, `none`, returning `null` when the nearest valid target is > 25 m away; `route(via)` as A* between consecutive points.
- **`PlaceIndex`** (`src/sim/api/places.ts`): `landmarks()`, `landmark`, `poi`, `poisNear(x, z, r, kind?)`, `poiPos`, `pedRoute(a, b)` (decimated, ≤ 64 points, on the topology graph), `findPlace(text, limit)` over landmark and POI labels (prefix and token match); the geocoder (`sf-geocode.v1`) plugs in at M02c2.
- **Boundaries and portals** (`02-world-maps.md` §4.1–§4.2; DL-691). Runtime only; M02a2 bakes the `portals` and `shoreNodes` tags.
  - `src/sim/api/boundaries.ts`:
    - County line: a soft wall on each `county-line` portal edge, with a static barrier collider and the toast `world.boundary.countyLine` ("Leaving SF — coming soon") once per approach. It also writes a fog-ramp factor to the env snapshot: 1 at 150 m from the line, falling linearly to 0.4 (fog far plane × 0.4) at the line. `src/client/boundary-fog.ts` applies it to M02b2's fog uniform.
    - Play bounds: `config/world.bounds.json` `play` plus 50 m is an invisible capsule wall for ground bodies; cameras and aircraft clamp at `maxY` 600 m.
    - Water soft wall: the pure rule `waterWall(state, depthM, ticks)`, using water depth from `WorldQuery` (bake sea level minus ground). At > 0.6 m depth it returns `stall` after 1.5 s (90 ticks), then `respawn` at the nearest road-adjacent `ShoreNode` through `TeleportApi.request('respawn', {x, z, yaw})` (yaw from the node's outward normal, reversed). Vehicles wire to it in M03a1.
  - `src/sim/api/portals.ts`: one runtime entry per `WorldIndex.portals` row, `state` `closed` by default. `portal.ggb` and `portal.baybridge` have a drivable deck up to a closure set piece. `setPortalState(id, state, by)` moves a portal to `sealed` by closing its `edges` through `TrafficControl.closeEdges(edges, by)`; `release(by)` restores it. `sealedPortals()` lists the sealed ids. `open` is S03's (scripted finales).
  - Prefabs: `assets/prefabs/portal.{ggb,baybridge}.json` (C06 prefab, kind `portal`, closure set piece with collider, render placeholder `prop/graybox.portal-closure` in the M01b1 precedent) and `prop.county-barrier` (static collider only).
  - **Swim** (C01 Control state 800; the M01b1 deferral that M03a1 did not take): `packages/engine/src/sim/control/swim.ts`. The on-foot owner hands over to `Swim` when water depth at the capsule exceeds `player.default`'s chest height. Swim then fades and respawns at the nearest `ShoreNode` through the port below (no swimming locomotion in v1, `01-engine.md` §2.4).
  - The engine reaches all of this only through `WorldBoundsPort` `{portals(), setPortalState, release, sealedPortals, nearestShore(x, z, roadAdjacent), waterDepth(x, z)}`. The port is defined in `packages/engine/src/sim/world/index.ts` (engine-owned, not a C08 shape) and registered with the `WorldApi` through the sim-module hook, so `@dv/engine` never imports `@dv/world`. M03c seals portals through it.
- **`WorldApi` wiring** (`src/sim/api/world-api.ts`): `createWorldApi(store, index, clock)` returns `{graph, traffic, map, teleport, query, places, resolver}`; registered through the engine sim-module hook (`SimModule` on `SimRuntimeEnv.modules`, M02b1, DL-651) so `packages/engine/src/sim/world/` exposes it to S01 code and tests; `__dvBridge.world.teleport(id)`, `route(a, b)`, `resolve(lat, lng)` in QA builds.

## Effort
1 Builder session; about 34 code files (27 + boundaries, DL-691); risk **medium**. Teleport placement against slabs and prisms and the topology hand-off in A* are the main time sinks; the boundary rules are small pure functions on top of `TeleportApi` and `TrafficControl`.

## Risks & fallbacks
- **Topology graph and lane graph disagree at chunk borders:** route on topology first, refine the resident part with lanes, and splice at the first resident node; log the rule.
- **Teleport into an unbaked area of `synth`** (water strips): AC-7 allows the precondition message for a non-landmark target; every §7 landmark has a land pad and an arrival pose on `synth` (DL-652), and the real `sf` landmarks get arrival poses from M02a2's operator bake.
