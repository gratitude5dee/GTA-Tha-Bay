# M02a1 — bake-core

## Goal
After this session `tools/map` is a real bake pipeline for the parts of San Francisco that do not need roads: S0 fetch with a pinned `raw.lock`, S2 terrain, S3 water, landuse and street trees, S4 buildings, the S13 pack writer (`p0` and `l0` `DVWP` v1 packs, `data/world-index`, attribution, provenance) and the gates of those stages. Three areas exist: `sf` (the real city; network; baked and published by the operator), `civic-excerpt` (a committed 1 km² source excerpt around City Hall; proves byte-reproducible bakes in CI) and `synth` (offline seeded sources in the same raw formats over the whole `play` box; the full-size CI world for M02b1–M02d2). `pnpm map:pack` turns a bake into C07 manifests with provenance and publishes it to a `dir:` or `r2:` target. Roads, landmarks and POIs come in M02a2.

## Requirements
- R-MAP-01: real footprints (DataSF `ynuv-fyni`), real terrain (Terrarium), real coastline and landuse (OSM, DataSF parks) baked in the ENU frame of C08.
- R-IP-01: open data only, with per-pack provenance (`origin: derived`, ODbL/PDDL source stamps, DL-161) and an attribution file; Google 3D Tiles and Mapbox are never sources (F-055, F-056).

## Scope in
- **CLI** (`tools/map/src/cli.ts`, extended): `map:fetch [-- --area sf|civic-excerpt] [--archive] [--fixture]`, `map:build [-- --area sf|civic-excerpt|synth] [--stage <ids>] [--force] [--repro]`, `map:pack [-- --bake <bakeId>] --target dir:<path>|r2:<env>`. `--stage` takes a comma list of `fetch,terrain,water,buildings,pack` (later stages add ids). `--repro` runs the selected stages twice with `--force` into two output directories and fails if any output byte differs.
- **Areas** in `config/world/sources.json` (schema in `tools/map/schemas/`): dataset ids and URLs (`02-world-bake.md` §2), licence and attribution text per source, and per-area gate parameters. Source ids and labels are neutral (`sfmta-stops`, `sfmta-patterns` for `i28k-bkz6`/`9exe-acju`, credit SFMTA): no source id, label, attribution text, code comment or fixture written outside `spec/` and `research/` contains `BART`, `Muni` or `Caltrain`, which M03b2 adds to the `ip:strings` `words` (DL-664 part 2). `sources.schema.json` rejects any URL whose host is `mapbox.com` or ends in `.mapbox.com` (Mapbox, F-056) or is `tile.googleapis.com` (the Photorealistic 3D Tiles host [research/repos-world.md, Verified facts], F-055), and S0 refuses the same hosts at fetch time (DL-676). The Geofabrik URL and the `osmium` commands are recorded there exactly as the operator first runs them (U-W1); `map:fetch` prints them before running.
- **S0 fetch** (`tools/map/src/sources/`): Socrata paging `$order=:id&$limit=45000&$offset=…` for `ynuv-fyni`, `j2bu-swwd`, `gtr9-ntp6`, `tkzw-k3nq` (streets, EAS and the SFMTA transit stops and patterns `i28k-bkz6`/`9exe-acju` are fetched here too, used from M02a2); Terrarium z15 tiles over `stream` ∪ `play` and z12 over `far`; OSM via `osmium extract` with the two bboxes of §2 and export to GeoJSON lines. Output `.cache/map/raw/<area>/` plus `raw.lock.json` (one tool-internal `RawSourceLock {id, url, retrievedAt, sha256, rows, licence}` per source: URL, retrieval date, SHA-256, row count; DL-666). For `sf`, the lock is committed as `content/world/raw.lock.sf.json`; `--archive` uploads the snapshot to `tha-bay-private-staging` (rclone, keys from `R2_PRIVATE_ACCESS_KEY_ID` / `R2_PRIVATE_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY`: a separate R2 API token scoped to `tha-bay-private-{staging,prod}`, because M01d's token covers only the media buckets; DL-287). Socrata requests send the optional app token from `SOCRATA_APP_TOKEN` (shell environment) when set; absent, they run unauthenticated. Without network, without `osmium` or with `DV_MAP_OFFLINE=1`, it prints `SKIP map:fetch <source>: <reason>` and exits 0.
- **`synth` sources** (`tools/map/src/sources/synth.ts`, `config/world/synth.json`): seeded generator that writes the same raw formats S0 writes (Socrata-shaped JSON rows, Terrarium PNG tiles, OSM-shaped GeoJSON lines) for: a land rectangle inside `play` with water strips north and east, plus one land pad per `02-world-bake.md` §7 landmark whose ENU point lies outside the rectangle or within 40 m of its water (`landPads` in `config/world/synth.json`: id, x, z, a square of at least 160 m, as an island or a pier strip joined to the rectangle; Alcatraz, Yerba Buena Island, Treasure Island, the bridge seeds, Pier 39, Fort Point and Crissy Field among them; DL-652), so every landmark arrival point is on land, smooth hills (City Hall post 17.6 m), 41 rectangular neighbourhood polygons carrying the 41 real names of `02-world-bake.md` §8, parks, and buildings by density band (a downtown band at the C08 rule-20 limit of 1,500 per chunk, ≤ 200 elsewhere). Its stamps are `origin: authored`, licence `LicenseRef-DeeVerse-Owned`; synth packs are QA-only and never enter `assets/assets.lock.json`.
- **`civic-excerpt`:** committed raw files in `tests/fixtures/map/civic-excerpt/` (footprints, two Terrarium tiles, an OSM GeoJSON extract, parks, neighbourhoods, its own `raw.lock.json` and `README.md`). The OSM reader (`sources/osm.ts`) keeps only the tag keys of the `02-world-bake.md` §2 OSM row (plus `restriction` and member roles on `type=restriction` relations), and the fixture cut writes only those keys, so `name`, `network`, `operator` and `brand` never reach the committed extract or the outputs (DL-664 part 2). `map:fetch -- --area civic-excerpt --fixture` cuts the real 1 km² around City Hall when the session has network; otherwise the Builder generates it with the synth generator restricted to that square and the README and stamps say `synthetic: true` (the operator re-runs the real cut later; Builder decision).
- **S2 terrain:** rules 1–5 of `02-world-bake.md` §4 S2 (Terrarium decode, 4 m bilinear posts, water clamps, piers). Edge posts come from one global grid, so neighbours are equal by construction; `sampleHeight` from `@dv/shared`.
- **S3 water, landuse, trees:** §4 S3 rules (coastline chaining, rings > 0.25 km², DP 4 m, landuse classes); street trees from `tkzw-k3nq` into `l0` TREE records.
- **S4 buildings:** rules 1–5 and 7–8 of §4 S4 (text height columns parsed, blend, clamp, ring cleaning, CCW, vertex caps, OSM gap fill ≤ 5 %, kinds, roofs, palette by `hash(sf16_bldgid)`). Rule 6 (landmark proxies) writes `proxyOf = 0` until M02a2 seeds landmarks. Over-limit chunks (> 1,500 buildings) fail the gate; nothing is split.
- **S13 pack and index:** `p0` sections HEIGHT, STRINGS, PRISM, GROUND; `l0` sections BUILDING, LANDUSE, TREE (LAMP and FURNITURE empty until later stages). Packs only for `z=7` tiles that intersect land or piers. `bakeId` = SHA-256 of canonical `{sourceHashes, toolVersion, configHashes}` (C08 rule 16); `worldSeed` = first 4 bytes. `data/world-index` validated against `tools/map/schemas/world-index.schema.json` (all §5 required properties; `landmarks`, `pois`, `portals`, `transit` may be empty until M02a2, whose `map:verify` enforces the v1 counts). `WorldIndex.sources` holds C08 `SourceStamp` records verbatim (`{id, url, retrievedAt, sha256, licence, attribution}`), built from the `RawSourceLock` entries with `attribution` filled from `sources.json` (DL-666). `config/world.bounds.json` regenerated and unchanged. Outputs in `dist/world/<bakeId>/` with `bake-report.json` (counts, gates, sizes, stage times).
- **Gates** (`tools/map/src/verify/gates.ts`), parameters per area: S0 row counts; S2 City Hall height 10–25 m, minimum ≥ −130 m, shared edge posts equal; S3, inside the area box, `area(land ∩ union)` within ±3 % of `area(union)`, where `union` is the union of the 41 neighbourhood polygons (the county land mask of S9 `county-line` portals), with `area(land \ union)` reported in `bake-report.json` and not gated (the `play` box reaches past the county line into Daly City, Colma, Brisbane and the Marin Headlands; ±3 % is a design value calibrated by AC-9, U-W14; DL-655); S4 baked DataSF buildings ≥ 98 % of the `ynuv-fyni` row count in `raw.lock.json` before proxies (dropped ≤ 2 %); OSM supplement ≤ 5 %, each addition listed by OSM id in `bake-report.json`; S13 every pack ≤ 4 MiB (warn > 2.5 MiB) and no key-like string in outputs.
- **Attribution:** `config/attribution.world.json` generated from `sources.json` and the committed raw locks only (`{id, text, licence, licenceUrl, source, surfaces}` per source), so it is reproducible offline; each bake copies the entries of its area next to its packs. The Terrarium `text` is the 3DEP credit followed by the registry citation "Terrain Tiles was accessed on DATE from https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles" [research/repos-world.md §4], DATE being the UTC `YYYY-MM-DD` of the Terrarium `retrievedAt` in the area's committed lock (`content/world/raw.lock.sf.json` for `sf`; until AC-9 commits it, the `sf` entry omits the citation and the generator warns). Which datasets feed the tiles over the bake boxes (bay and ocean bathymetry included) and the z12 far box, and what credits they require, is UNVERIFIED (U-W16): AC-9 records them, and any extra credit enters `sources.json` only through an Architect DL entry before M02c2 AC-14 releases `sf` (DL-672 part 2).
- **`map:pack`:** writes one C07 `AssetManifest` per pack (`world_pack/p0-<x>-<y>`, `world_pack/l0-<x>-<y>`, `data/world-index`, variant `default`), one `ProvenanceRecord` per pack (`origin: derived`, `tool: tools/map@<version>`, `derived_from` = the source stamps' SHA-256s, each stamp its own `open_data` record; synth stamps `authored`), and `dist/world/<bakeId>/catalog.fragment.json` (tool-internal `{bakeId, entries: AssetManifest[]}`, entries sorted by ref); then copies the blobs through the M01d uploader. Target `r2:<env>` (operator, `sf` only) additionally writes the lock entries to `assets/world/sf.lock.json` (the entry shape of `assets/assets.lock.json`, validated by the same lock schema) and the provenance records under `assets/` for a PR. It never writes `assets/assets.lock.json` or the committed pointers, so every build without `DV_WORLD` keeps the M01f gray-box fixture; `sf` reaches a deployed build only through M02c2's world release (DL-670).
- **Catalog merge** (`tools/assets/src/publish.ts`, extended; DL-542): `assets:publish -- --env staging --target dir:<path> --pointer-out <dir> --fragment <catalog.fragment.json>`. `--env staging|prod` stays required for every target, as the M01d CLI has it (`tools/assets/src/cli.ts` exits 2 with its usage line without it); with a `dir:` target it only names the environment in the log line (DL-665). `--fragment` is accepted only with a `dir:` target (otherwise it exits non-zero with `--fragment needs a dir: target`). After `assets:verify` passes on the lock set, each fragment entry is checked (key pattern of M01d AC-3, variant `default` only, `sha256` equal to the blob already under `<path>`, `world_pack` ≤ 4 MiB); the entries are then added to the catalog entries before `buildId` and the manifest hash are computed. A fragment ref equal to a lock ref (for example the M01f `world_pack/p0-64-64`) replaces it in this catalog only, and the replacement is printed. The manifest goes to `<path>/manifests/` and the merged pointer only to `--pointer-out`. The committed pointers (`apps/web/public/asset-catalog.json`, `services/edge/src/generated/asset-catalog.json`) and `assets/assets.lock.json` are never written on this path. Equal inputs give byte-identical output. `AssetCatalog` and `CatalogPointer` keep their C07 shapes.
- **QA build hook** (DL-665 part 2; M01e BD-002, BD-010): when `DV_WORLD=<bakeId|area>` is set (an area name means that area's latest bake), M01e's QA media-local step `apps/web/scripts/qa-media.mjs` (run by the root `build` as `pnpm --filter @dv/web run media:local` before `vite build`; it does nothing unless `VITE_DV_QA=1`) first runs `pnpm map:pack -- --bake <bakeId> --target dir:apps/web/media-local`, then its existing publish (`pnpm run assets:publish -- --env staging --target dir:apps/web/media-local --pointer-out apps/web/media-local`) with `--fragment dist/world/<bakeId>/catalog.fragment.json` added; either command failing fails the build. M01e's `media-local` Vite plugin then copies `apps/web/media-local/` to `dist/media-local/` in `closeBundle`, unchanged. QA builds read the pointer at `/media-local/asset-catalog.json` (M01e catalog client, BD-002), so the merged pointer reaches M02b1's boot rule with no root pointer override: `dist/asset-catalog.json` stays the committed pointer in every build (M01d AC-10) and `/api/v1/boot` `assetCatalog` keeps the committed key. Without `DV_WORLD` the step publishes the lock set alone, whose manifest has no `data/world-index`. Production builds skip the step and ignore `DV_WORLD`. If the merged M01e differs from `origin/claude/build-m01e` (7905997), the Builder extends the merged form after the spec-sync check of DL-690 and records it as a BD row.
