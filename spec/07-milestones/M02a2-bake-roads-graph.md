# M02a2 — bake-roads-graph

## Goal
After this session the bake produces a drivable, walkable city. Stages S5–S10 run between terrain and packing: the directed road graph with classes, one-way rules and layers (S5), merged junctions with movements, conflicts, restrictions and signal programs (S6), road grading into the shared heightfield with bridge slabs and tunnel hoods (S7, so S4 buildings now sit on the graded grid), the pedestrian graph (S8), the seven tag types and the spawn tables (S9), and the landmark seeds, POI table, transit nodes and the transit routes asset `data/transit-routes.v1` (S10, DL-664). The always-resident topology asset `map_data/sf-graph.v1` is written. `pnpm map:verify` runs the S14 gates on a finished bake, and `pnpm map:landmarks`, `pnpm map:pois` and `pnpm map:resolve` exist for authors. The offline areas (`civic-excerpt`, `synth`) gain roads, so CI can prove graph connectivity and routes; the real `sf` gates (hero edges, landmark snapping to OSM) are proven on the operator's bake. Stage S11 also writes the `l1`, `l2` and `far-bay-*` packs that M02b1 streams and draws (DL-546).

## Requirements
- R-MAP-01: the real street grid (OSM geometry, DataSF `3psu-pn9h` names, classes and one-way), hills graded into drivable roads, bridges as slabs and portals, landmarks seeded at their real places.
- R-MAP-02: the backdrop and far-LOD packs (DEM ring, massing, placeholder skyline cards) are baked (S11).
- R-MAP-04: signal programs, sidewalks and crossings, spawn capacities and density profiles that the living city (M03b1, M03b2) consumes.

## Depends on
- **M02a1:** `tools/map` areas, S0/S2/S3/S4/S13 stages, `raw.lock`, gates, `map:build`, `map:pack`, the synth generator.
- Contracts: C08 1.0 (`02-world-contracts.md` §1–§5: `RoadEdge`, `Junction`, `SignalProgram`, PED, TAGS, SPAWN sections, limits of rule 20).

## Scope in
- **Pipeline order** (`02-world-bake.md` §1): S0 → S2 → S3 → S5 → S6 → S7 → S4 → S8 → S9 → S10 → S11 → S13 → S14. `--stage` accepts `roads,junctions,grading,ped,tags,places,lod`.
- **S5 roads** (`02-world-living.md` §1.1–§1.3; `config/world/roadclass.json`): drivable `highway` classes only, excluded DataSF layers (`PAPER*`, `PSEUDO`, `PRIVATE`, `STREETS_PEDESTRI`, `UPROW`); DataSF `classcode` overrides the OSM class on `cnn`-matched segments; OSM `lanes`, `maxspeed` override defaults; `widthM` formula; parking sides. One-way: test both orientations of DataSF F/T against OSM on matched segments, adopt the one with higher agreement, fail S5 if neither exceeds 90 % (design value on `sf`, U-W15; DL-669), and write both agreement ratios and the 20 worst-disagreeing segments (`cnn`, OSM way id) into `bake-report.json`; DataSF wins on matched active `STREETS` segments. Layers give an `i8` level; crossings without a shared node never connect. Ids per C08 rules 8–9.
- **S6 junctions** (§1.4–§1.6): merge nodes within 25 m of one intersection (≤ 8 approaches); controls (`Signal`, `AllWayStop`, `MinorStop`, `Yield`, `Roundabout`, `DeadEnd`); `type=restriction` removes movements; U-turns only at dead ends; turn classes; connector curves with exact ops; conflicts within 2 m, ≤ 12 per movement. Signal programs: phases by heading (4-way 2, 3-way 2, 5+ 3), green 20 s, amber 3 s, all-red 1 s, Arterial through 28 s with 16 s cross, `cycleTicks = Σ(green + amber + clear) × 60`, `offsetTicks = hash(junctionId) mod cycleTicks`, pedestrian walk = parallel green minus 5 s.
- **S7 grading** (`02-world-bake.md` §4 S7 rules 1–6; `config/world/bridges.json` with Golden Gate deck 67 m, Bay Bridge 58 m, generic overpass terrain + 5.5 m × layer): class-order processing, 24 m moving average, 40 % slope clamp, smoothstep blend, slabs 4 m × 1.0 m with steps ≤ 0.16 m, tunnel corridors lowered and covered by hoods. Sloped deck strips stay deferred (DL-104; proposal P2).
- **S8 pedestrian graph:** sidewalks derived from road width on both sides where OSM has no `sidewalk=no`, crossings at junction approaches (`movement` = the parallel straight movement), paths and plaza lattices; per-tile largest component ≥ 85 % (warning).
- **S9 tags and spawn** (§4 S9 table and formulas): `hidingSpots`, `pursuitBreakers` (from `content/world/pursuit-breakers.json`, snapped ≤ 12 m), `chokePoints`, `jumpZones` (≤ 3 per chunk), `portals` (GGB north at 37.8263, −122.4793; Bay Bridge east at the Yerba Buena Island mouth; Ferry Building; `county-line` per drivable edge leaving the neighbourhood union), `shoreNodes` every 40 m, `teleportNodes`. Spawn: `trafficSlots`, `pedSlots`, parked slots at 7 m pitch, 15 % empty by hash, capped at 256, ordered by hash so a prefix is a thinning. Profiles from `content/world/districts.json` (10 districts + `outer` + `stage` + 41 neighbourhood slugs, `02-world-bake.md` §8), each naming a profile that the bake turns into the C08 `densityProfile` index.
- **Density profiles (DL-555):** this milestone creates `config/world/density.json` with `profiles`, an ordered array whose position is the baked `densityProfile`: `cbd`, `cbd-tech`, `nightlife`, `civic-dense`, `tourist-dense`, `industrial`, `residential`, `residential-park`, `sparse`, `freeway`, `beach`, `none` (the names of `02-world-living.md` §7 and the §8 district table; `none` for `stage`), each with `name` and `perKm` (peak pedestrians per sidewalk km for the S9 `pedSlots` formula; design values, recorded as a BD row). The order is append-only: a new profile goes at the end, and a removal or reorder needs an Architect DL entry and a full re-bake. The schema `tools/map/schemas/density.schema.json` (C08 config schemas live in `tools/map/schemas/`) allows the keys later milestones add (M02b2 `lampPitchM`; M03b1 hour curves, weather and tier factors). S9 fails on a district profile name missing from `profiles`.
- **S10 places:** `content/world/landmarks.json` with the 37 seeds of §7 (id, lat/lng, class, district, role, `replaces` footprint ids filled by the bake, `arrival` pose computed on ground ≥ 3 m from water and prisms; on `synth` every landmark stands on M02a1's land pads, so a landmark there without an arrival pose fails S10, DL-652); landmark proxies set `proxyOf = index + 1` in `l0` BUILDING records (S4 rule 6). `content/world/pois.json` with ≥ 150 seeds (`02-world-bake.md` §9: 37 landmark POIs, 8 transit stations, 41 neighbourhood hubs, ≈ 20 parks and plazas, ≈ 45 fictional businesses and anonymous homes), each bound to a ped node ≤ 25 m. Transit nodes: the five Muni Metro station positions of fact N4 plus authored BART and Caltrain seeds (snapped; U-W5), giving exactly 8 `bay-rail` nodes (`fastTravel: true`; Embarcadero, Montgomery, Powell, Civic Center, 16th St, 24th St, Glen Park, Balboa Park, `02-world-living.md` §5); `content/world/cablecars.json` street chains checked within 6 m (U-W6). `content/world/teleports.json` (sandbox list = the 37 landmarks).
- **S10 transit routes (DL-664)** (`tools/map/src/stages/s10-transit.ts`): writes the tool-owned data asset `data/transit-routes.v1` (canonical JSON, schema `tools/map/schemas/transit-routes.schema.json`, packed by `map:pack` and resolved through the catalog like M02c1's `data/district-grid.v1`; no C08 field or `TransitNode` kind is added). Contents, all in ENU metres: `metro` surface splines from OSM `railway=light_rail|tram` ways (tunnel sections dropped); `bus` patterns from `9exe-acju` `shape`s snapped to the road graph as `EdgeId` lists, with the `i28k-bkz6` stops of each pattern bound to `(edge, s)` within 25 m; `ferry` lines from OSM `route=ferry` ways, else the authored Ferry Building ↔ Treasure Island and Ferry Building ↔ Alcatraz lines; `cablecar` splines from the snapped `cablecars.json` chains; and 3 `air` corridors (SFO and OAK approaches, 600–1,500 m). Authored ferry fallbacks and corridors live in `content/world/transit-lines.json` (design values, recorded as a BD row; `config/world/transit.json` is created later by M02c1, so it cannot hold bake input). Line and stop names are `NameRegistry` keys, never source strings. The record's `derived_from` lists the OSM, DataSF and `content-world` stamps, and the asset is in the ODbL `database.assets` scope below. Route positions come from the real data; only names and liveries are fictional (R-MAP-01).
- **`map_data/sf-graph.v1`:** nodes, edges (from, to, length, class, flags, layer), no geometry; layout chosen here and written as a comment at the top of `packages/shared/src/world/graph-codec.ts` (pure `encodeGraph`/`decodeGraph`), listed in the PR as a C08 clarification proposal (like M01f's layout notes). Packed by `map:pack` as `map_data/sf-graph.v1`.
- **Gates and tools:**
  - `map:verify [-- --bake <id> | --area <id>]` runs every S14 gate on a finished bake (latest bake of the area by default; with no bake it prints `SKIP map:verify: no bake in dist/world` and exits 0): SCC ≥ 98 % of drivable edges, no `lanes = 0`, every signal junction has a program with ≥ 2 phases, a route from the City Hall edge to every portal, edges per chunk ≤ 600, out-degree ≤ 8, max road grade ≤ 40 %, ≥ 98 % of OSM signal nodes within 25 m of a junction (the one-way 90 %, SCC 98 % and signal 98 % thresholds are design values on `sf`, U-W15; DL-669; each gate row carries its measured ratio), chunk limits of rule 20, world-index counts (landmarks ≥ 25, POIs ≥ 150, exactly 8 `bay-rail` transit nodes), transit routes (≥ 1 `metro`, ≥ 2 `bus` patterns with ≥ 2 stops each, ≥ 1 `ferry`, exactly 3 `air`; ≥ 1 `cablecar` on `sf` only; every bus edge id exists in `sf-graph.v1`; DL-664), and, for `sf` only, the hero edges of §1.8 drivable and connected. Writes the gate table into `bake-report.json`.
  - `map:landmarks [-- --bake <id> | --area <id>]`: schema, unique ids `lm.<slug>`, classes, districts, lat/lng ↔ ENU agreement with the §7 table within 1 m; with an `sf` bake, each landmark within 120 m of its OSM feature (a failing seed is corrected from OSM in `landmarks.json`, never waived).
  - `map:pois [-- --bake <id> | --area <id>]`: schema, counts by kind, unique ids, fictional business names (shared matcher of `names:lint`, plus the `ip:strings` matcher `compileStringMatcher` over `config/ip-denylist.json`, so a denylisted brand fails; DL-664), no EAS address as a home; with an `sf` bake, the id prefix equals the neighbourhood slug of the point and each POI binds to a ped node ≤ 25 m (warnings on `synth`).
  - `map:resolve -- --lat=<y> --lng=<x> [--area <id>]`: prints edge id, `s`, distance and chunk key.
- **Synth and excerpt roads:** the synth generator (M02a1) adds OSM-shaped highways on a grid with arterial spines, signals at arterial crossings, one layered overpass and one short tunnel (so slabs and hoods are exercised), DataSF-shaped street rows with `cnn` and `oneway`, and county-line portals at its land edges; for transit (DL-664) one OSM-shaped `railway=light_rail` line along an arterial spine, two `9exe-acju`-shaped bus patterns with `i28k-bkz6`-shaped stops, one `route=ferry` line across the north water strip between two land pads, and a land pad (DL-652 rule) for each Bay Rail seed off the rectangle; per-area gate parameters disable the `sf`-only gates.
- **S11 LOD and far** (`tools/map/src/stages/s11-lod.ts`, `s11-far.ts`; moved from M02b1, DL-546): `l1` (DP 3 m, drop h < 6 m outside d1/d5, 16 m posts, road lines class ≤ 4), `l2` (massing ≥ 20 m, 64 m posts, class ≤ 2), `far-bay-dem` (z12 at 256 m posts plus land mask), `far-bay-massing` (buildings ≥ 15 m as boxes in 1 km cells), `texture/far-bay-cards` as a **placeholder atlas** (flat silhouettes in PNG, codec `png`; the rasterized cards are M10d2's art pass). Packs ≤ 4 MiB, reproducible, packed by `map:pack` beside `p0`/`l0`.
- `nightly.yml`: `pnpm map:build -- --area synth && pnpm map:verify -- --area synth`.
- **Real brands in the denylist (DL-664):** this is the first milestone that ships brand-bearing names, so it adds `Waymo`, `Tesla`, `Transamerica`, `Salesforce`, `Oracle Park` and `Chase Center` to the existing `words` list of `config/ip-denylist.json` (whole-word, case-insensitive through `compileStringMatcher`; no schema change) [research/legal-ip-likeness.md §6; `02-world-bake.md` §7 rule 6]. A legitimate occurrence outside the path allowlist (for example a data-source credit) takes an exact-match `exceptions` entry with a reason, never a path exception (M00b rule).
- **Authored inputs in the database (DL-677):** from this milestone `content/world/{landmarks,pois,districts,pursuit-breakers,cablecars,teleports,transit-lines}.json` feed the same `p0`/`l0` packs, `data/world-index`, `data/transit-routes.v1` and `sf-graph.v1` as the ODbL data. No proprietary data enters these assets (R-S02-04).

## Risks & fallbacks
- **One-way agreement below 90 %, signal match or SCC below 98 % on real data** (U-W15; design values, never measured on `sf`): the gate fails by design; record the measured ratios and the worst segments in `notes.json` and report Blocked on U-W15; do not lower a threshold without an Architect decision. `synth` and `civic-excerpt` stay gated at the design values (AC-3, AC-4), because their data are generated or fixed.
- **Tunnel hoods collide wrong** (R-S02-01): short tunnels become open cuts (corridor without hood), long ones stay; the drive tests come in M03a1.
- **SCC below 98 % on `sf`** from OSM gaps: print the disconnected components with `cnn`s; fix only via `config/world/roadclass.json` exclusions or authored connector edges in `content/world/`, each listed in the report.
