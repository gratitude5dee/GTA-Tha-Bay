Original text for the M00c spike. Every brief describes an invented character and an invented stage. No real people, no franchises, no artists, no "in the style of". Sections are read by heading name (`@brief:<name>` in `plan.json`).

## runner_sheet
A character turnaround sheet for an invented courier called Spike Runner: an adult athletic woman in her late twenties, short dark curly hair, warm brown skin, round yellow-framed glasses, a teal windbreaker with a white zip, grey cargo trousers, orange running shoes, a small black waist bag. Four full-body panels in one row, equal width, left to right: front view, left-side view, back view, right-side view. Each figure stands in a neutral T-pose, arms straight out to the sides, palms down, feet shoulder width apart, the same scale and the same ground line in every panel. Plain flat light grey background with no shadows, no text, no labels, no panel borders. Even soft studio light. Illustration look: clean semi-realistic game concept art with simple shapes and flat colour fills.
