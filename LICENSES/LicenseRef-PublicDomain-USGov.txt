U.S. Government works in the public domain (USGS 3DEP and SRTM inside the AWS Terrain Tiles)

LicenseRef-PublicDomain-USGov marks data authored or produced by the U.S. Government that is in the
U.S. public domain. In this repository it covers the elevation data of the AWS Terrain Tiles
(Terrarium PNG, s3://elevation-tiles-prod): USGS 3D Elevation Program (3DEP) and SRTM, tiled by
Mapzen (a Linux Foundation project).

The registry page has no licence URL. It is the citation page:
https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles

Credit text shown in the game: "3DEP data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey", followed by the two lines the Terrain Tiles
attribution document requires for the datasets behind the bake boxes: "United States 3DEP (formerly NED) and global GMTED2010
and SRTM terrain data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey." and "Global ETOPO1 terrain data U.S. National Oceanic and
Atmospheric Administration" (ETOPO1 is NOAA public domain; https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md,
checked 2026-10-19).
Citation: "Terrain Tiles was accessed on <date> from https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles".

Sources: research/repos-world.md section 4 (https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/,
https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md and
